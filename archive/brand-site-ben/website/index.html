      About

      Who is behind Pace & Publish, and how this guide was made.

      The team

      Pace & Publish is a fictional motorsport news brand, created and run by a student marketing team as a project for MK-680. The team built the brand from the ground up, from its name and voice to its colors and look, and runs its Instagram account, where it shares quick updates on what has been officially announced across F1, MotoGP, WEC, IndyCar, NASCAR, and other series. Every post is held to the same standard: a named official source, no rumors or speculation, and a tone that is professional, succinct, and genuinely interested in the sport. This website grew out of the same goal. Where the Instagram account follows the news, this guide gives new fans the background they need to enjoy it.

      How this site was made

      This website was built with help from Claude, an AI model made by Anthropic. Working from the team's plan and a requirements document developed with the team, Claude researched and wrote the guide's entries, chose the openly licensed photos, and wrote the site's code. The team set the direction at every step, including which pages to include, which series to cover, and the colors, type, and design, and reviewed the results along the way.

      Every fact on the site comes from a cited source, not from the AI's memory, and each entry links to the sources it uses. AI can still make mistakes. If you spot an error, let us know on Instagram.
