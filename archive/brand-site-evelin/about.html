About Pace & Publish

Motorsport news in one place: F1, MotoGP, NASCAR and WEC results, schedules and recaps.

Header: 2024 Dutch Grand Prix (file photo) · Photo: Steffen Prößdorf · CC BY-SA 4.0 · Wikimedia Commons (links open in a new tab)

01 Our story

Every series. One feed.

Fans who follow more than one racing series usually have to check several websites and apps to stay current. Pace & Publish is a news aggregator that brings those series together in one place.

Every post is written in our own words and built on confirmed facts only, taken from each series' official website and cited every time.

02 Who we're for

Multi-series fans

Fans of several racing series who want their news in one place instead of across multiple sites and apps. They usually read us first thing after sleeping through a race or practice session, or after work when they want a quick update on the day.

Content needed: audience demographics

The brand files do not yet give an age range, location or named personas for our audience.

03 Voice

How we sound

  • Authentic

    Every caption is in our own words. We never copy or lightly reword another outlet's article.

  • Professional

    We report what happened without taking sides, and never show a favorite team or driver.

  • Intriguing

    Each post leads with a fact that gives fans a reason to open it.

04 Standards

Editorial standards

  • Official sources only. Facts come from formula1.com, motogp.com, nascar.com and fiawec.com, and every story names its source and date.
  • No rumors as facts. A rumor is mentioned only when it is clearly labeled as unconfirmed.
  • No predictions. We never predict the outcome of a session, race, championship or season.
  • Current information. Team names, line-ups and schedules are checked on the official site on the day a post is drafted.
  • Neutral. Series always appear in the same order, F1, MotoGP, NASCAR, WEC, and no team, driver or series gets extra emphasis.
