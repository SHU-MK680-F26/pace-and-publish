About

Motorsport news for fans of more than one series

Photo: MotoGP, 2007 Australian motorcycle Grand Prix · Tom Reynolds, CC BY 2.0, via Wikimedia Commons

Who we are

Pace & Publish is a motorsport news brand on Instagram (@paceandpublish). This website is its home on the web: news, race results and the race calendar for four series together, so you don't need several apps and sites.

Who it's for

Fans who follow more than one racing series. You might check in first thing in the morning after sleeping through a race or session, after work for a quick update on the day, or at the start of a race week to see what's racing and when. Most of you read us on a phone.

The four series we cover

  • F1 Formula 1 · source: formula1.com
  • MotoGP MotoGP · source: motogp.com
  • NASCAR NASCAR · source: nascar.com
  • WEC FIA World Endurance Championship · source: fiawec.com

Where our facts come from

Only the four official series sites above. Every news item and race on this site names its source. If a fact isn't confirmed on an official site, it isn't on Pace & Publish.

Our rules

Confirmed facts only · No predictions · No favorites.
