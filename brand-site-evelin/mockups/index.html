Three homepage directions

Design comps for approval, not the live site. They use real verified Pace & Publish headlines. Photos marked "Candidate" are openly licensed Wikimedia Commons images, shown here for preview only and not yet approved or downloaded. Yellow "Photo needed" boxes are gaps no suitable licensed image could fill.

A

Grand Prix

Cinematic magazine. A full-bleed car hero, oversized numbering, a diagonal red band and an asymmetric photo mosaic. Closest to formula1.com and McLaren.

 B

Telemetry

Timing-screen dashboard. Stories listed like a timing tower, the car framed by technical lines, large figures and a grid canvas. The most distinctive and data-led.

 C

Paddock Journal

Luxury editorial. A floating pill navigation, a restrained hero, alternating chapter spreads and plenty of negative space. Closest to Porsche and Ferrari.
